NEEDS VS.

 WANTS

<!-- Slide number: 9 -->

Needs
Needs are the things that are most important and must be prioritized.

For example: food, water, shelter, and the love and support of your parents are all needs.

<!-- Slide number: 10 -->

Wants
Wants are things we think we need but can live without.

For example: mobile phones, accessories, trendy toys, and gadgets provide temporary joy and pleasure, but life goes on even without them.

Thriftiness

and frugality

<!-- Slide number: 14 -->

Thriftiness and frugality are the habits of spending time, money, and resources wisely to save and conserve the environment.

<!-- Slide number: 15 -->

THRIFTS

<!-- Slide number: 16 -->

![](Picture9.jpg)

1. Be Thrifty

Buy and use only what you need. Avoid mistakes so you can save paper, pencils, and time.

<!-- Slide number: 17 -->

2. Handle things with care

![](Picture3.jpg)

Take care of your school supplies, toys, and gadgets so they will last, and there will be no need to buy them too frequently.

<!-- Slide number: 18 -->

3. Recycle and reuse

Do not throw away the things that can still be used in other ways.

![](Picture3.jpg)

<!-- Slide number: 19 -->

![](Picture4.jpg)
4. Invest

Use your time to finish your school work. Instead of playing online games, go out and play with your neighbors and friends.

<!-- Slide number: 20 -->

5. Finish your food and do not waste any

![](Picture3.jpg)

Get only what you can eat so that nothing will be wasted.

<!-- Slide number: 21 -->

![](Picture4.jpg)

6. Trade

Trade the things you no longer use but are still in good conditions.
