What is Prayer?
Prayer is our way of talking to God. Since God is a spirit, we cannot send Him a letter, chat with Him online, or call Him on our phones. But the amazing thing is that we can talk to Him anytime and anywhere through prayer.

<!-- Slide number: 9 -->

What is Church?
Church is a special place where people go to pray, worship God, and learn about His love.

<!-- Slide number: 10 -->

What is Religion?
Religion is what people believe about God and how they worship Him. It teaches people how to pray, be kind, love others, and do what is right.

What do we pray for?

<!-- Slide number: 12 -->

Give thanks

We pray to tell God that we are thankful for the blessings He gives us every day.

<!-- Slide number: 13 -->

2. Ask

We pray to ask God for help with our needs.

<!-- Slide number: 14 -->

3. Praise and adore

God provides us with all our needs even though we do not know why. So, we pray not just to ask but also to appreciate God’s goodness.

<!-- Slide number: 15 -->

4. Be sorry

We say sorry to ask for forgiveness and promise not to do it again.
