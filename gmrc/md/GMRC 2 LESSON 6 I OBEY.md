LESSON 6: I OBEY (OBEDIENCE)

Prepared by: Ms. Danica Marie Vivas, LPT

<!-- Slide number: 10 -->

OBEDIENCE

Obedience is doing what one ought to do as instructed by parents and people in charge and the law. It is respect and obedience in action.

<!-- Slide number: 11 -->

To obey is to follow the words or orders of our parents, teachers, and elders in charge of us.

<!-- Slide number: 12 -->

Why should you obey?

Children should obey because:
it is for their saftey and well-being;
it builds better relationships between elders and children;
it is an effective training to make good decisions;

<!-- Slide number: 13 -->

Why should you obey?

Children should obey because:
it leads to a peaceful family and  community; and

it is our duty and obligation to our family, community, and county.

<!-- Slide number: 14 -->

Being an obedient child is being responsible in doing one’s duties and obligations to the family and community.

<!-- Slide number: 15 -->

RIGHTS

These are the things that every person should have and enjoy.
 Example: The right to learn, play, and be treated with respect.

<!-- Slide number: 16 -->

DUTIES

These are the things that we are expected to do.

 Example: A learner’s duty is to listen to the teacher and follow school rules.

<!-- Slide number: 17 -->

RESPONSIBILITIES

These are the things we should do properly and responsibly.

 Example: Taking care of school things, helping at home, and keeping the surroundings clean.

<!-- Slide number: 18 -->

Few things you have to be responsible for:

Love and respect your parents, family, and community.

Help your community and cooperate with them.

<!-- Slide number: 19 -->

Few things you have to be responsible for:

Obey community rules and regulations.

Take care of yourself.

Study diligently.
