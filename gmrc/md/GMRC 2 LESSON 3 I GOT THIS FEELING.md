Five Basic Emotions

<!-- Slide number: 10 -->

Happiness

Is a feeling of joy. It is also related with cheerfulness, excitement, admiration, enjoyment, and glee.

<!-- Slide number: 11 -->

Sadness

Is the opposite of happiness. It is another word for loneliness. We all feel sad sometimes and that is normal and perfectly fine.

<!-- Slide number: 12 -->

Anger

Is a feeling often related with irritability, tension, and anxiety.

<!-- Slide number: 13 -->

Fear

Is a natural response to danger and threat. It is another word for being scared.

<!-- Slide number: 14 -->

Disgust

Is a strong feeling of dislike triggered by something unpleasant, such as touching something prickly or tasting something nasty.

CATCH YOUR FEELINGS

<!-- Slide number: 17 -->
Here is a plan to help you avoid hurting yourself or others. Remember to CATCH your feelings:

<!-- Slide number: 18 -->

Be Calm

Do not act right away to negative emotions. Take slow and deep breaths when you feel frustrated.

<!-- Slide number: 19 -->

Accept your
feelings

Be honest about how and why you feel that way. Acknowledge your emotions and express them sincerely and truthfully.

<!-- Slide number: 20 -->

Try doing
something

Counting 1 to 100, working on an art project, or listening to a calming music can also help ease anger and anxiety.

<!-- Slide number: 21 -->

Call for help

Expressing your feelings and asking for help allows those around you, like your parents and siblings, to support you better.
