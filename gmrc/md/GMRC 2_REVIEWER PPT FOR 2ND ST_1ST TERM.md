LESSON 6: I Obey (Obedience)

Prepared by: Ms. Danica Marie Vivas. LPT

<!-- Slide number: 18 -->

Obedience
Obedience is following the words and advice of our parents, teachers, elders, and those in charge of us.

<!-- Slide number: 19 -->

Being obedient is being responsible in doing one’s duties and obligations to family and community.

<!-- Slide number: 20 -->

EVERY CHILD HAS A DUTY TO:

take care of himself or herself and stay away from harm and danger;
study diligently, improve his or her talents, and skills, and develop oneself; and
love and respect his or her country, all its symbols, and take care of government properties.

love and respect their parents, family and community;
help the community and cooperate with them;
obey community laws and rules;

<!-- Slide number: 21 -->

REMEMBER:

By being a responsible member of the family and community, one develops and shows love for the country.

LESSON 7: I Can (Self-Confidence)

Prepared by: Ms. Danica Marie Vivas. LPT

<!-- Slide number: 23 -->

Ability

An ability is something you can do well or learn to do well.

<!-- Slide number: 24 -->

TALENT

A talent is a natural skill or strength that you may develop through practice.

<!-- Slide number: 25 -->

EXAMPLES OF ABILITIES AND TALENTS:

Playing sports
Helping others
Speaking
Building or creating things

Drawing
Singing
Dancing
Reading
Writing

<!-- Slide number: 26 -->

OUR FAMILY CAN HELP US DISCOVER OUR ABILITIES AND TALENTS BY:

Encouraging us to try new things.
Supporting our interests.
Giving helpful advice.

Praising our efforts.
Giving us opportunities to practice.
