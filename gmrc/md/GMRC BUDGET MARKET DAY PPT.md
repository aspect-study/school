BUDGET MARKET DAY

Prepared by: Ms. Danica Marie Vivas

### Notes:

<!-- Slide number: 8 -->

Assessment Overview

Budget Market Day is a hands-on performance activity where learners use play money to purchase items from a classroom market while staying within a given budget. Through the activity, learners demonstrate their ability to:
·Identify needs and wants, read and compare prices, add the prices of items, determine if they have enough money to buy their chosen items, calculate simple change, make wise spending decisions, and demonstrate the importance of saving money.
