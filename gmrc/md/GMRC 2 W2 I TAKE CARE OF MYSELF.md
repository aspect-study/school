How do I Take care of myself?
Hand-washing is one of the best ways to keep germs away. Germs can stick to your hands when you play, touch toys, or when you meet other people. If too many germs enter your body, then can make you sick. But thanks to our IMMUNE SYSTEM, we become well again.

IMMUNE SYSTEM

The Immune system is made up by network of tissues and organs, all working together to protect the body from viruses, bacteria, and other invaders that can cause damage and diseases.

                Eating junk food can make you feel weak and tired. However, if you eat healthy and nutritious foods, you will also feel healthy and well.

2. Physical Fitness

                Exercise strengthens our heart and make other body organs strong. Children of your age should do at least one hour of moderate to vigorous activities daily, such as running, swimming, dancing, and sports.

3. Quality Sleep

           Sleep is very important part of health and wellness. While we sleep, our bodies are in the most relaxed state. Children need 10 to  11 hours of sleep to maintain good health and proper development.

4. Proper Hygiene

           Hygiene is about taking care of yourself and your surroundings to stay clean and healthy.  Observing proper hygiene helps us stay healthy and safe.

5. Mental Health

             Mental health is just as important as physical health. It affects how we handle stress, emotions, and challenges. The best way to handle stress is by maintaining a healthy and balanced life.
