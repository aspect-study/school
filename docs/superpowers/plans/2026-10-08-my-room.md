# Wardrobe 5a: My Room Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** She walks into My Little House, into one room that grows with her medals (4×4 → 5×5 → 6×6). In a 🛠️ Decorate
view from above she places furniture on squares (tap an item, tap a square, ↻ turn, 📦 put away) and buys new pieces,
also sold at Tito Tasyo's workshop, a fourth Shop Plaza stall. The room has a study desk (Hoot's ❓ Ask me! at home), a
trophy shelf (one trophy per subject, coloured by its medals), the mirror (moved inside), earned items she can never
buy, and her sister can visit it, view only.

**Architecture:** Pure logic first, each in its own file with Node tests: `furniture.js` (catalog), `room.js` (squares,
fits, auto-place, move, turn, put away, repair), `trophies.js` (colour and bubble line), `house.js` (`house_v1` read,
save, buy, earned unlocks, desk count, `seen`). Then the 3D: `room3d.js` draws the room and one builder per catalog
item; `decorate.js` is the Decorate view; `carpenter.js` puts the catalog in `stall.js`. `world-main.js` moves her in
and out (the sparkle hop), hides the outdoors, clamps the camera, and wires the desk, shelf, mirror, nook, visit and
celebrations. Sync, family peek, backup and the parent panel each gain one small rule.

**Tech Stack:** Plain ES5 browser scripts (IIFE, `root.World3D`), Three.js r149 (toon materials, code-built shapes),
Node's built-in test runner (`node --test`), headless Chrome e2e (`node tests/e2e/world-e2e.js`,
`node tests/e2e/sisters-e2e.js`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-08-my-room-design.md`

## Choices this plan makes where the spec is silent

These follow the spec's intent; the owner can change any of them at the final review.

1. **Coordinates.** The room's south-west corner is `ORIGIN = { x: 200, z: 200 }`. Square `(c, r)` has its centre at
   `x = 200 + (c + 0.5) * 2.4`, `z = 200 - (r + 0.5) * 2.4` (rows run north, toward −z, as everywhere in the world).
   An item's `(c, r)` is its south-west square; its footprint is `w × d` squares (`w` along c, `d` along r), swapped
   when `turn` is odd.
2. **Rugs and kept-clear squares.** Kept clear means nothing *stands* there. A rug lies flat, so it may cover the
   walkway, the mirror square and the door square (a door mat), never the desk square. This is what lets the starter
   round rug sit in the middle of a 4×4 room.
3. **The trophy shelf hangs above furniture height** on the west wall over rows 1-2 (wall squares `w1`, `w2`); the floor
   squares below it stay usable. She taps a trophy in 3D from anywhere in the room (a raycast, like tapping her pet).
4. **The window seat's alcove** is the north wall square `n5`, which only exists at 6×6, so growing never displaces a
   wall item. The alcove is one square wide.
5. **Wall squares** are named `{ side, i }`: `n` and `s` count `i` west to east, `w` and `e` count `i` south to north.
   The `wall` hint scans `n` (i ascending), then `e`, then `w` (both i descending, north first), then `s`.
6. **Scan orders.** `wall-side`: rows from the north (`r = size-1 … 0`), columns from the west, turn 0 then turn 1; the
   first spot that fits and touches a wall wins. `middle`: every spot that fits (turn 0 and 1), the one whose
   footprint centre is nearest the room centre; ties go to the earlier spot in the `wall-side` scan order.
7. **"N to go"** on a trophy bubble is the lessons with no medal yet.
8. **The nudge inside** opens Mayor Mimi's nudge bubble (her words and Go ▶) without walking her into the room; Mimi
   stays outdoors.
9. **The desk asks at most 3 questions per room visit** (counted by answers given); a 4th Study shows "That's 3
   questions for this visit! Come back later. 🌟".
10. **Visit button:** the first sister (family order) whose peek carries a readable `house_v1`. A sister who has a
    `house_v1` but never went in shows the starter room. Her room size is `seen.size` (4 when missing).
11. **Merge ties:** `room` and `seen` from the side with the later `at`; on a tie, the one whose JSON text sorts
    larger, so both merge orders agree.
12. **Growth popup** shows only when `seen` exists and the new size is bigger than `seen.size` (never on the very first
    entry).
13. **Tito Tasyo's preview** stands on a small round display stand in front of her (between her and the stall camera)
    and turns slowly, as the counter preview the spec describes.

### Task 2: Squares (`room.js`)

**Files:** create `web/world/room.js`, `tests/world3d-room.test.js`; wiring as Task 1 (after `furniture.js`).

API (all pure; `cat` is `Furniture`, passed in so tests can use small fakes):

```js
SQ = 2.4; ORIGIN = { x: 200, z: 200 }; WALL_H = 5.5;
SIZES = { grade5: [[0, 4], [15, 5], [40, 6]], grade2: [[0, 4], [10, 5], [25, 6]] };
sizeFor(grade, medals) -> 4 | 5 | 6
DOOR = [1, 0]; WALK = [1, 1]; MIRROR = [2, 0]; DESK = [0, 0];
WALL_TAKEN(size) -> ['s1', 's2', 'w1', 'w2'] plus 'n5' at 6
center(c, r) -> { x, z };  squareAt(x, z, size) -> { c, r } | null
footprint(it, p) -> [[c, r], ...]                  // floor items; p = { id, c, r, turn }
wallSquares(it, p) -> ['n3', 'n4']                 // wall items; p = { id, side, i }
fits(cat, room, p, skip) -> bool                   // room = { size, placed }; skip = index ignored (moving)
autoPlace(cat, room, id) -> p | null               // choices 5 and 6
place(cat, room, id) -> room | null                // a new room with p appended
move(cat, room, k, c, r) / moveWall(cat, room, k, side, i) -> room | null
turn(cat, room, k) -> room | null                  // wall items never turn
putAway(room, k) -> room
spots(cat, room, k) -> [{ c, r }] | [{ side, i }]  // where item k fits with its current turn (the glow)
repair(cat, room, size, has) -> room               // drops not-owned, unknown, off-room, kept-clear, overlapping
start(cat, size) -> { placed, wall, floor, stars } // the starter room
solid(cat, room) -> [{ x, z, hx, hz }]             // standing items as walk-blocking boxes (rugs and wall items skip)
```

Fit rules: inside `0 … size-1`; never the desk square; a standing item never on the door, walkway or mirror square
and never on another standing item; a rug never on another rug; wall items on free wall squares of their own side,
never on `WALL_TAKEN(size)`, a 2-wide item on `i` and `i + 1`.
