# Wardrobe 3c: ride sounds and the stall cha-ching

Date: 2026-10-07. Follows 3b (`2026-10-07-world-sounds-design.md`, built), which added `world/sfx.js`, the clips in
`web/assets/sounds/world/` and `tools/world-sounds.js`. 3c reuses all of it.

## Decisions (user, 2026-10-07)

- **Scope A:** playground ride sounds + a cash-register sound for stall buys. **Not** button taps, **not** effect
  sounds (bubble pops, sparkles): taps get noisy, and effects mostly happen during prop/emote moves that already have
  sounds.
- Sub-project 4 of the wardrobe (particle trails + auras) is **excluded** by the user.
- **Rides follow the motion (A):** the slide whooshes once as she starts down; each swing creaks as she passes the
  bottom; the see-saw thumps at each touchdown; the merry-go-round sounds as it starts and as it slows; every ride has
  a start sound and a landing sound.
- **Cha-ching (A):** stall buys play the same `Fx.purchase()` cash register as the lobby's real-reward shop.
- **Approach A:** cue moments worked out from the ride maths in `rides.js` (`Rides.cues`), reported by `play.js`
  through a new `o.cue(name)` hook, played by `world-main.js` through `sfx`.
- Clips picked by ear on a second audition page (scratchpad, session-only).

No cuts or speed changes. 6 new files (the slide shares the cartwheel clip): the folder goes from 50 to **56** files.
Gains: `swing` 0.4 (it plays 3 times), the rest 0.5. Cooldown: the default 0.15 s (the closest repeats are 1 s apart).

The k values come from `rides.js`: the swing angle is `0.7·sin(4πk)` (zero at k = ¼, ½, ¾), the see-saw tilt is
`0.3·sin(4πk + π/6)` (extremes at k = (⅓ + n)/4), the slide starts down at k = 0.25, and the merry-go-round eases to a
stop.

## `rides.js`: cues

```
Rides.CUES = {
  slide:  [[0, 'ride-start'], [0.25, 'slide-go'], [1, 'ride-land']],
  swings: [[0, 'ride-start'], [0.25, 'swing'], [0.5, 'swing'], [0.75, 'swing'], [1, 'ride-land']],
  seesaw: [[0, 'ride-start'], [1/12, 'seesaw-bump'], [4/12, 'seesaw-bump'], [7/12, 'seesaw-bump'], [10/12, 'seesaw-bump'], [1, 'ride-land']],
  merry:  [[0, 'ride-start'], [0, 'merry-start'], [0.7, 'merry-slow'], [1, 'ride-land']]
}
Rides.cues(id, from, to) → the cue names with from < k ≤ to, in table order ([] for an unknown ride)
```

- `play.js` `start(id)` reports `cues(id, -1, 0)` (the k = 0 cues). Each `tick` reports `cues(id, kBefore, kNow)`, so a
  long frame that jumps over several moments still reports each once, and nothing repeats across frames. The last
  frame (k = 1) reports `ride-land` before she is put down.
- `play.js` stays free of audio: `o.cue` is optional (`if (o.cue)`).

## Stall cha-ching

`world-main.js` `openStall` → `onBought`: `sound('allRead')` becomes `sound('purchase')`. `sound(name)` already calls
`root.Fx[name]()` inside a try; `Fx.purchase()` is silent when Sound is off (`Fx.muted()`), like every Fx sound. The
other world chimes (quick travel, the maker's Done, Jesus' hug, sending a cheer, the boss chest, a door in reach) stay.

## Out of scope
