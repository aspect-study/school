HARDWARE

These are the physical components of a
computer. These are tangible. We can see
and touch them. The monitor, keyboard,
computer case, CPU, mouse, speaker,
and printer are example of hardware.

SOFTWARE

These are the intangible aspect of the
computer system. These are the
programs in our computer. The
programs consist of instructions that tell
the hardware how to perform a task.

SOFTWARE

Examples of software are the operatig
system like Windows, graphic programs
like Microsoft Paint, word processors like
Microsoft Word, and computer games
and apps.

PEOPLEWARE

They are the people interacting with the
computer system. They are the users of
the computer. Example of peopleware are
out teacher, classmates, friends, families,
and ourselves using the computers.
