1. Improving one’s life
through education
Through education, you
get to know and
understand yourself
better, your purpose, and
your worth.

2. Practicing values and virtues as a powerful
way to show respect for human life.
On one hand, values are like a
personal compass, guiding you
toward good behavior. They help you
make wise choices in life. Virtues, on
the other hand are good behaviors
and traits that people admire.

3. Accepting corrections, from parents,
teachers, and other caring individuals is a
sign of humility.
As a growing child, it is
essential to be open to
feedback and suggestions
for improvement.
