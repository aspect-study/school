Ordinal Numbers
These are numbers that
tell the position or order
of something.

Ordinal Numbers in Symbols
First = 1st
Second = 2nd Third = 3rd

Ordinal Numbers in Symbols
For ordinal numbers ending
with 0, 4-9 we add “th” in the
symbols after the last digit.
Example:
10 = 10th
14 = 14th
17= 17th
