# Windows Desktop Activity — Voice-Over Script
Grade 2 · Keep a slow, clear pace. Move the mouse to each item AS you say its line.

---

## OPENING

Hi! My name is ______. I am in Grade 2.
Today I will show you the Windows desktop.

---

## PART 1: Explore the Desktop
*(Point the mouse at each one as you say it.)*

1. I am looking for the **wallpaper**. Here it is — this is the big picture on my screen.
2. I am looking for a **desktop icon**. Here it is — this little picture opens a program.
3. I am looking for the **taskbar**. Here it is — the long bar at the bottom.
4. I am looking for the **Start button**. Here it is — the window logo on the taskbar.
5. I am looking for the **Search box**. Here it is — I type here to find things.
6. I am looking for the **notification area**. Here it is — it shows little icons like the wifi and the sound.
7. I am looking for the **date and time**. Here it is — at the corner of the taskbar.

---

## PART 2: Customize the Desktop

Now I will change my wallpaper.

1. First, I will **right-click** on the desktop... and I will click **Personalize**.
2. I can see many buttons here. I will click **Background**.
   Now I will pick a new wallpaper from the recent images... I will choose this one.
3. Now I will go back to the desktop. Look — this is my **new wallpaper**!

---

## PART 3: Taskbar Navigation and System Settings

1. Now I will click the **Start button** and look for **Microsoft Edge**.
   I found it! I will click it to open it. Here is Microsoft Edge. Now I will close it.
2. Next, I will use the **Search box** to search for **Paint**.
   I will type P-A-I-N-T... and open it. I will not close it yet.
3. Now I will **pin Paint to the taskbar**. I will right-click the Paint icon down here... and click **Pin to taskbar**.
4. Now I will close Paint. Look — the Paint icon is still on the taskbar. It is pinned correctly!
5. Next, I will use the **Search box** to search for **date and time**, and I will click it.
6. Before I change anything, I will remember my time zone. My time zone right now is **______**.
7. Now I will change my time zone to **Osaka, Sapporo, Tokyo (Japan)**.
   The time in Japan now is **______**. Here it is on my screen!
8. I am done showing it. Now I will change my time zone back to **______**, just like before.

---

## CLOSING

That is all! Thank you for watching my Windows desktop activity.

---

### Notes for the parent
- **Blank in step 6 and 8:** the original time zone (for the Philippines it is usually *UTC+08:00 Kuala Lumpur, Singapore* or *Taipei* — check the screen before changing it).
- **Blank in step 7:** Japan is **1 hour ahead** of Philippine time. Read the time straight off the clock on screen.
- Record the desktop AND your daughter in the same frame, as the instructions require.
