GRASPS FOR PERFORMANCE TASK
Your situation is to explore your family and community,
identify important people and places, practice healthy
habits and good values, and organize your learning into a
creative portfolio. Your required outputs are: (1) a creative
and organized Family and Community Portfolio with simple
sentences, pictures, colors, and decorations; (2) a
community map showing and labeling important places such
as your home, church, wet market, school, and barangay hall;
and (3) a short oral presentation where you confidently
explain your portfolio and map.

GRASPS FOR PERFORMANCE TASK
Your goal is to express ideas about yourself and your family
using words and simple sentences, create a map that shows
important geographical features and places in your
community, and demonstrate values such as valuing oneself,
sincerity, being prayerful, prudent, obedient, and self-
confident. Your portfolio and presentation will be evaluated
based on the accuracy and completeness of information,
correct use of words and simple sentences, clarity of the
community map and labels, demonstration of healthy habits
and good values, creativity and neatness, clear speaking,
confidence, participation, following directions, and
submission on time.

Performance Task
(PETA)

Identify the members of your family and write 2-3
simple information about them. Look at important places in your
community. Talk about the healthy habits you practice at home.
Also, include pictures, colors, and decorations to make your
portfolio creative, colorful, neat and attractive.
Step 2:
Draw a simple map of your community. Organize the
information you gathered about your family and healthy habits
and write simple sentences about them.

Performance Task
(PETA)
Step 3:
Draw a map of your community and label the
important places around your home. Include your family,
church, wet market, school, and barangay hall. Write simple
sentences describing each place, the people you can see
there, and what they do.

Performance Task (PETA)
Step 4
: Practice and Master your Presentation. Read your
sentences aloud and practice saying the words clearly.
Practice how you will explain your map and portfolio to your
classmates.
Step 5
: Present your portfolio to the class. Share how your
family helps you stay healthy and feel good about yourself.
Tell the class what you learned about your family and
community.
