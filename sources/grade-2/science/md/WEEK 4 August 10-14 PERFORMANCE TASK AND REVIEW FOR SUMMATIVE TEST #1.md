My Mini Mystery Box

Instruction : Each student per group will bring 1 object from their home that is safe under the 3 states of matter.

Materials needed :
Shoe box, coloring materials,  pencil

<!-- Slide number: 5 -->

Steps

Step 1: Design your shoe box using the materials.
Step 2: Each member bring 1 safe object from their house for their group that is under the 3 states of matter.
Step 3:Place it inside of the shoe box
Remember : No one should know the object you put inside of your mystery box

<!-- Slide number: 6 -->

Steps

Step 4:Present the 5 objects in front of your class and teacher
Explain it in front of the class. Tell:
Name of the object
Describe the characteristics of the object
Tell where you found it in the school
Explain why it is that state of matter

<!-- Slide number: 7 -->

Guide Question

Is this object found at school, at home, or both?
How can you describe the object's characteristics using your senses?
 (What does it look like? How does it feel?)
How do you know that your object is matter?
