How can you enrich these gifts and talents?

<!-- Slide number: 14 -->

Acknowledge your gifts and be proud of what you can do.
You have talents because God gave them to you. To be proud of one’s gifts does not mean being boastful. It is simply owning your talents and skills and being responsible for them.

<!-- Slide number: 15 -->

2. Practice to improve

When you practice, you learn new ways of doing things better. You also learn from your mistakes and avoid repeating them.

<!-- Slide number: 16 -->

3. Use your talents and skills properly.
Talents and skills are given to you by God so that you enjoy your life better when you use them properly. The best way to use your gifts properly is to share them with others joyfully.
