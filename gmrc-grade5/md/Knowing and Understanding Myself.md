Growing and
Embracing
Change

What is Change?
Change is a natural and exciting
part of life.
It helps us grow and learn new things.
Example: A caterpillar changes into a
butterfly.

Changes We Experience
Physical changes – becoming
taller and stronger.
Emotional changes – learning to
understand and manage feelings.
Personal changes – discovering new skills,
talents, and interests.

Growing Through Challenges
Changes help us discover our
identity, values, and beliefs.
Challenges at home or school build
resilience (the ability to recover from
difficulties and keep going).

How to Value Oneself

1. Obey Your Parents
Listen to parents and
elders.
Learn from their
experience.
Respect their advice.

2. Change Undesirable Attitudes
Accept correction.
Replace bad habits
with good ones.
Build confidence
through positive
attitudes.

3. Do Something Good for Yourself
and Others
Discover your talents.
Join school and
community activities.
Help other people.

4. Learn to Appreciate
Be thankful for your
blessings.
Practice gratitude
every day.
Appreciate even the
simple things

5. Be Aware of Yourself
Understand your
feelings and emotions.
Identify why you feel
upset or angry.
Practice self-control
and resilience.

“ Growth begins when we
embrace change”

Follow Your Heart
Directions:
Draw a large heart on a short bond paper. Inside
the heart, write a short letter to yourself.
Encourage yourself by recognizing your strengths,
accepting your weaknesses, and reminding
yourself to keep growing and believing in yourself.
Then, decorate your heart with colors and designs
that represent who you are.

You may:
Encourage yourself.
Remind yourself of your strengths.
Express your feelings.
Share your hopes and dreams.
